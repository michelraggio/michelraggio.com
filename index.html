A little about me

I'm an entrepreneur and startup operator with a background spanning investment banking, corporate finance, product management, and early-stage ventures.

I started my career in investment banking before making the jump into startups – moving from Europe to Asia and from financial analysis to product management and entrepreneurship. Since then, I've founded and led early-stage companies, worked across product development, strategy, fundraising, and operations, and served as CFO of a hospitality startup.

I'm drawn to technology and the challenge of turning ideas into products and businesses.

I'd describe myself as tenacious and proactive. I like figuring things out, getting things moving, and tackling problems where there isn't an obvious playbook.

Always happy to connect with founders, investors, builders, and people working on interesting problems.
